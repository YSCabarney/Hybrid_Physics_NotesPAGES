QUESTION 12 points

Scalar or vector?

Four lines from a match report. Exactly one of them describes a vector quantity. Choose it.

A scalar needs a number and a unit. A vector needs a number, a unit, and a direction — drop any one of the three and it is no longer a vector.
  • AThe keeper’s goal kick traveled 55 m.
  • BThe match lasted 94 minutes.
  • CThe free kick curled 22 m toward the far post.
  • DThe squad covered 108 km combined over the 90 minutes.
Then, in one sentence: what is missing from the other three?
QUESTION 24 points

Distance and displacement on the pitch

A midfielder starts at the halfway line. She sprints 25 m in a straight line toward goal, then immediately jogs 10 m straight back along the exact same line and stops.
Both legs happen along one straight line, so no triangles are needed — just add and subtract carefully.
a.What total distance did she cover? Include the unit.
b.What is her displacement? Include the unit and the direction.
c.Explain in one sentence why the two answers are not the same.

QUESTION 36 points

Average speed and average velocity

During a four-leg shuttle drill a player covers 48 m of ground in 8.0 s. When she stops, she is 12 m from her starting cone, toward the sideline.
Four legs out and back. The ground she covered and her net change in position are two different numbers — which means two different formulas.
a.Calculate her average speed.
b.Calculate her average velocity.
c.A teammate says “those should be the same number.” Explain why they are not.

QUESTION 54 points

Units, conversions, and labeling

A winger’s average velocity down the touchline is measured at 8.5 m/s, toward the corner flag.
1 m/s ≈ 3.6 km/h ≈ 2.24 mph. Converting units changes the number in front, never the motion itself.
a.Convert her velocity to km/h.
b.Convert her velocity to mph, rounded to the nearest whole number.
c.Is 8.5 m/s, toward the corner flag a speed or a velocity? How do you know?
d.The GPS vest also reports that she covered 9.4 km in the match. Is that a scalar or a vector?
